Billing FAQ

What happens to my API key when I upgrade?

Your existing key keeps working — its limits, models, and features update immediately, no rotation needed.

Can I downgrade or cancel?

Email api@careerstudiomax.com to cancel or change tiers. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Is my card information stored on your servers?

No — card details go directly to Stripe via their SDK and never touch our backend. We only store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs.

What if my bank requires extra verification?

We support 3D Secure (SCA) automatically — if your bank needs to verify the payment, you'll get a secure popup from your bank before the upgrade completes.
